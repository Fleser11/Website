About Me

A recently graduated Computer Science major from Michigan Technological University, I've had a passion for solving problems for as long as I can remember — the kind of curiosity that turns into staying up too late taking something apart just to see how it works. Outside of school and work, I'm usually outside hiking, exploring, or snowmobiling in the winter, tinkering with some project of my own, or catching up with friends over a good book.

How did I get here?

  1. 2nd Grade

    Discovered a love for programming and solving hard problems — an interest that's guided my trajectory ever since.

  2. Freshman Year, High School

    Joined the robotics team, FRC Team 3546 — my first real taste of building something technical alongside a team.

  3. Senior Year of HS – First College Semester

    Got my first real introduction to the engineering world, working in an electrical engineering research lab on new product development.

  4. Michigan Tech

    Declared Computer Science and joined Innovative Global Solutions (IGS), starting on the infant incubator project's controls subteam — embedded C++ and circuit board design.

  5. Internships

    Simulation tooling and hardware prototyping at Phase 1 Engineering, then applied machine learning for medical imaging as an EEDP intern at GE HealthCare.

  6. Spring 2025

    As IGS Project Lead, helped the incubator team win the 1st Place Innovation Award at the Design Expo.

  7. January – April 2026

    Served as President of IGS, leading the entire 30+ member enterprise across all of its client projects.

  8. April 2026

    Graduated Summa Cum Laude with a BS in Computer Science, and was recognized with the Audience Choice Award at the Undergraduate Research Symposium for my Rules as Code research with Dr. Wallace — work supported by SURF and URIP grant funding.
