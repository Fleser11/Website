Connor Fleser Portfolio

My name is Connor Fleser, I am a current Computer Science undergraduate student at Michigan Technological University.

I have long been interested in technology and programming. In fact I've said that I want to be a coder since I was in 2nd grade.

These days, however, I've found that what I really enjoy about Computer Science and programming is the problem solving component. Every time I am presented with a task or problem in front of me I am immediately excited to begin to break down the problem and start brainstorming solutions.

In my opinion this is also one of the most rewarding aspects of Computer Science, to be able to be presented with a problem and work with these basic tools upon which you can create a solution is nothing short of magical.

It is due to this fascination with problem solving and technology that has led me to where I am today. I am pursuing my Bachelors degree in Computer Science and am looking at getting my Masters here at Michigan Tech with their four plus one program to get an accelerated masters.

In this I am getting involved with research looking at formal modelling in software applications as well as taking time to develop skills related to software development and Computer Science theory.

Other interests of mine in the realm of Computer Science include Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence. I find the pure application of math to create machines capable of solving problems on our behalf to be an incredibly interesting and promising science.
